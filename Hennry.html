About Horrid Henry

Horrid Henry is the mischievous star of the famous children's book and TV series. He loves playing pranks, dislikes homework, and is always getting into trouble. His younger brother, Perfect Peter, is well-behaved, making the two complete opposites. Despite his naughty behavior, Henry is clever, funny, and always finds exciting adventures.
