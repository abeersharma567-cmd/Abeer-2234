About

Tanjiro Kamado is the main protagonist of Demon Slayer. After his family is attacked by demons and his sister Nezuko becomes a demon, Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps to protect humanity and find a cure for his sister. His determination, kindness, and mastery of Water Breathing and Sun Breathing make him one of the strongest Demon Slayers.
